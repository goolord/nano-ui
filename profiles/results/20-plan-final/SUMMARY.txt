nano-ui frame-allocation reduction plan: session report (final)
date: 2026-09-12 (Linux x86_64, GHC 9.14.1, -O2 -mavx2, RTS -N1 -A64m -T -I0)

Goal: >=70% fewer allocated bytes/frame on a fixed workload.

Outcome: 70% NOT met. Delivered 29.1% on the deterministic ui/runFrame
benchmark (455 -> 323 KB medium, 945 -> 670 KB large) and large widget
microbenchmark wins, with no test regressions and byte-identical draw
geometry. Phases 4 (dense storage) and 5 (layout reuse) were not
implemented; the remaining allocation is diffuse per-widget structural
overhead, which those rewrites must address.

Remaining bottleneck (measured this session):
  A standalone harness (see notes) measured the marginal per-widget
  allocation: ~2.4 KB (box), ~2.7 KB (label), ~3.3 KB (button), of which
  `addWidgetStyled` is ~1.2 KB. Module-scoped -fprof-auto splits that 1.2 KB
  across the do-block glue (Eff binds, captured closures, Response, Maybe)
  with no single allocation site -- i.e. it is diffuse structural overhead.
  An empty frame costs ~11 KB, ~20% of it Effectful runEff/env handling,
  ~17% writeDamage. The fixed cost and the ~60 small allocations/widget are
  exactly what Phase 4 (dense persistent slots) targets.

Attempted and rejected (measured no gain, reverted):
  - float-argument pushQuadF (GHC already elides the boxed Rect)
  - NOINLINE resolveInteraction diagnostic (allocation unchanged)
  - INLINE addWidget/addWidgetResp/addWidgetStyled (unchanged; 10x compile)
  - addWidgetIO top-level worker to keep the block out of the Eff closure
    (allocation unchanged)
  - manual set/clear replacing bracket_ in withArenaArraysSnap (no gain)

Deferred with rationale:
  - Phase 4 dense storage and Phase 5 layout reuse: architectural; the
    measured diffuse per-widget/frame overhead is their target.
  - Demo FPS-string formatting cache: affects only the debug HUD.
