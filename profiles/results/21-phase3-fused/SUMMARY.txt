Phase 3 (part 2): fused single-reservation rounded shapes
date: 2026-09-12 (Linux x86_64, GHC 9.14.1, -O2 -mavx2, RTS -N1 -A64m -T -I0)

Problem: every rounded fill made 9 separate arena reservations (1 centre +
4 side quads + 4 corner fans) and every rounded border made 8 (4 strips +
4 corner arcs). Each reservation re-read the arena caps/counts, re-created
a closure and re-checked capacity. Profiling put the corner fan/arc at
~9% of frame allocation and ~27% of paint CPU.

Changes (NanoUI.Draw):
- pushRoundedRectRaw now reserves once for the straight quads plus all four
  corner fans, writes them at running vertex/index offsets and commits the
  counts once. pushCornerFan removed.
- pushRoundedStroke now reserves once for the side strips plus the four
  corner arcs, in both the zero-radius and rounded branches.
  pushCornerArcStroke removed; shared pokeStripAt helper.
- Vertex order, index order and counts are byte-identical to the previous
  per-shape reservations (verified below).
