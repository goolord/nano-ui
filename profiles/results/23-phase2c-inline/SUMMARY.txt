Phase 2/3 (part 4): cross-module INLINE for resolveNodeFont
date: 2026-09-12 (Linux x86_64, GHC 9.14.1, -O2 -mavx2, RTS -N1 -A64m -T -I0)

resolveNodeFont (NanoUI.Frame.Paint.Types) returns IO (FontMetrics, Bool)
and was not INLINE, so its result tuple was heap-allocated at every call in
paintTextNode and paintWidgetForeground. Marking it INLINE lets the tuple
(and the discarded Bool) unbox at the call sites.

Measured ui/runFrame alloc: medium 323 -> 317 KB, large 670 -> 657 KB
(-30.5% vs 945 KB baseline). cabal test all PASS; geometry unchanged.
Other candidates tested in the same pass (measureText, treeDecodeStyle,
tableStripeColor, widgetVisualStyle, displayText) were allocation-neutral
or cost compile time and were reverted.
