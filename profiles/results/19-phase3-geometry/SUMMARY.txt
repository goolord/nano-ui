Phase 3: reduce CPU geometry bookkeeping (partial)
date: 2026-09-12 (Linux x86_64, GHC 9.14.1, -O2 -mavx2, RTS -N1 -A64m -T -I0)

Changes:
- Draw.hs: add loopIO, a strict numeric loop; replace the
  forM_ [0 .. n] enumeration lists in pushCornerFan, pushCornerArcStroke,
  pushRoundedStroke and pushLine.
- Paint/Widgets.hs: replace zip [0..] placements + forM_ in
  paintWidgetForeground with an explicit index loop (avoids the zip list).
- Layout/Arena.hs: withArenaArraysSnap uses bracket_ instead of bracket
  (no resource threaded to the cleanup).

Measured: ui/runFrame small 17 KB / medium 406 KB / large 844 KB --
unchanged from Phase 2. GHC already optimised the range-list loops in this
configuration, so these are allocation-neutral; the fold removes the
dependency on that optimisation and the bracket allocation. CPU timings
were not comparable this session (system load average rose from 1.3 to 2.9
during measurement).

Not done: fused single-reservation rounded shapes. The corner fans/arcs
allocate ~9% and consume ~27% of paint CPU, but fusing the reservation is a
vertex/index-layout change with real regression risk for every backend, and
was deferred rather than landed without a reliable CPU delta.

Verification: cabal test all PASS.
