nano-ui perf after Phase 3 (damage-clipped paint pass)
date: 2026-09-10 (Linux x86_64, GHC 9.14.1, -O2 -mavx2, RTS -N1 -A64m -T -I0)
vs Phase 7 (profiles/results/08-phase7-wrap/bench.txt)

changes:
  writeDamage now runs before paint (it had no paint dependency), so the
  frame knows its damage before emitting geometry. When the SDL present will
  be partial, runFrame sets the draw arena's current clip to the damage rect
  inflated by one logical pixel (to cover the runner's outward pixel snap);
  lowerNode's existing rect/clip early-out then culls off-damage subtrees and
  no vertices are emitted for them. Full-present frames (fresh retain,
  forced full, continuous present, window expose) keep painting everything.
  The SDL runner now calls ensureRetain before the frame and passes the
  full/partial decision through ctxPaintFull; finishDraw receives
  presentFull explicitly.

correctness (differential pixel test, temporary harness, removed after):
  120-frame interaction sequence (counter text changes, hover sweep, scroll,
  checkbox toggles) rendered two ways:
    - culled paint + damage scissor
    - full paint + damage scissor (forced via NANO_UI_FORCE_PAINT_FULL=1)
  screenshots are byte-identical -> culling emits exactly what the retained
  scissor path would have drawn. A 219-pixel delta vs a fully-repainted
  frame is pre-existing retained-mode behavior (the same delta appears with
  full paint + scissor), not introduced by culling.
  Both variants are deterministic across runs.
