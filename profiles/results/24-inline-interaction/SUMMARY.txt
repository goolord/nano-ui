Phase 3/4 boundary: aggressive cross-module INLINE of the widget hot path
date: 2026-09-12 (Linux x86_64, GHC 9.14.1, -O2 -mavx2, RTS -N1 -A64m -T -I0)

Root cause found: several hot functions returning records/tuples were not
INLINE, so GHC could not unbox their results or fold their common branches at
the per-widget call sites. Profiling charged the cost to their callers with no
locatable child.

Changes:
- b9bf0f4 INLINE resolveInteraction (biggest win)
- 162741b INLINE paintWidgetForeground
- e59024b INLINE fillStyledRect / strokeStyledRect / strokeRoundedBorder
- <next> INLINE paintWidgetBackground
